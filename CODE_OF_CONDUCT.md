# Code of Conduct

[По-русски](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a straightforward technical
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project work:

- staying on the technical substance;
- accepting that a reviewer may disagree with you and saying why;
- assuming the other person is acting in good faith until shown otherwise;
- respecting that many contributors work under real personal risk and may
  need to stay anonymous.

Things that do not:

- personal attacks, harassment, or insults;
- publishing anyone's identity, location, employer, or contact details
  without their explicit consent — in this project this is not merely
  rude, it can be dangerous;
- pressing a contributor to reveal where they live or which network they
  tested from;
- sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately through GitHub's report form on this
repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this
document, and may ban a participant for repeated or severe behaviour.
